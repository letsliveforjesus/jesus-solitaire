How to Play

  1. Tap a face-up card to select it.
  2. Tap another pile to move the selected card.
  3. Build the seven bottom piles in descending number order.
  4. Alternate red and dark cards in the bottom piles.
  5. Only a King can move into an empty bottom pile.
  6. Build the top foundation piles from Ace to King by matching theme.
  7. Tap the purple card pile to draw a new card.
