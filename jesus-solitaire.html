Play a Bible-themed version of classic Solitaire. Move cards in descending order and build each foundation from Ace through King.

How to Play

  1. Tap a face-up card, then tap where it should go.
  2. Double-tap a card for an automatic move.
  3. You can also drag and drop face-up cards.
  4. Tap a selected card again to clear your selection.
  5. Build tableau piles downward in alternating colors.
  6. Any face-up card may move into an empty tableau pile.
  7. Build each foundation from Ace to King by theme.
  8. Tap the purple deck to draw a new card.
  9. When the deck shows ↻, tap once to recycle cards.
  10. When all tableau cards are face up, tap Auto Finish.
